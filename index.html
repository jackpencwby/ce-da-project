K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

To classify a new point, KNN measures its distance to every stored example, picks the K closest ones and lets them vote. It has no weights to optimize — “training” simply means memorizing examples. Step through the learning process and watch the decision boundary take shape.

  1. 1Measure distanced(q,x) = √Σ (qⱼ − xⱼ)²
  2. 2Pick K nearestNK(q) = K smallest d
  3. 3Majority voteŷ = argmaxc #votes(c)
